# Cloud Providers and Locations

## Main providers
- **AWS** – `aws.amazon.com/regions`
- **Microsoft Azure** – `azure.microsoft.com/explore/global-infrastructure/`
- **Google Cloud** – `cloud.google.com/compute/docs/regions-zones`

## Key terms
`region` A geographic area with one or more data centers.  
`availability zone / zone` An isolated group of resources inside a region.  
`edge` Points of presence closer to users for lower latency and caching.  
`datacenter` A physical building that houses servers and storage.

## Healthcare-related note
When patient data, e-prescriptions, hospital integration, or government records are used, verify:
1. Region/residency requirements.
2. Compliance and accreditation options.
3. Business Associate Agreement (BAA) or equivalent where applicable.
4. Audit logging, encryption, and access controls.

Always link the current provider console and check for regional changes before designing a solution.
